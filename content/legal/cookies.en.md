# Cookies

We only use cookies the site needs to work. There are no advertising or tracking cookies, so we don't ask for consent. If we add analytics later, we will ask first.

## Necessary cookies

- **sb-…-auth-token** – keeps you signed in (Supabase). Lasts until you sign out or it expires.
- **NEXT_LOCALE** – the language you chose.

## Stored in your browser

- **Review draft** (sessionStorage) – so a half-finished review isn't lost. Cleared when you close the tab.

## Embedded badges

RateThatJob badges shown on other websites set no cookies and record nothing about visitors. We only count which website shows the badge.
